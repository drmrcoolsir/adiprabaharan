Coursework

Where the time is going

Linear Algebra core

Matrix similarity and diagonalization, nilpotent maps and kernel chains, orthogonal matrices and Gram–Schmidt. Work currently limited to that assigned by Imperial, but will persue personal research in the near future.

Group Theory core

Subgroups of the symmetric groups S₃ and S₄, worked through cycle decomposition and closure arguments.

Probability & Statistics core

Where the majority of my time is spent. Mostly probability puzzles, having learnt about joint CDFs, moment generating functions, probability generating functions, and random variables.

Applied Mathematics core

Applications of graphs and nodes. Conductance and current density, Laplace's and Poisson's equations, conformal mapping, and the electric circuit / random walk analogy (escape probabilities and harmonic functions in particular).
